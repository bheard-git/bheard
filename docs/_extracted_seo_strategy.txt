1. SEO Positioning (Locked)
Primary positioning phrase: “Mumbai's integrated branding and technology agency for hospitality, lifestyle and consumer brands.” Use consistently in homepage title tag, Organization schema description, and footer boilerplate.
Strategy: Do not compete on “social media marketing agency Mumbai.” Lead with the two-pillar (Brand + Tech) integrated positioning and the hospitality vertical, where the client roster (Accor, Radisson, Novotel, ITC, Goa Tourism) is the differentiator.
Build order: /industries/hospitality-luxury and /services/tech-solutions/ai-chatbots-agents ship first — low-competition, high-intent pages with genuine proof. Rank these, then use internal links from them to lift harder head terms.

5. Technical Requirements for the Developer
Rendering & indexation
Server-render or statically generate every page. All copy, headings, FAQs and client names must be present in the initial HTML response — not injected client-side after load.
Noindex all Vercel staging domains. bheard.vercel.app and bheard-in.vercel.app must serve an X-Robots-Tag: noindex header (or password protection). If they are already indexed, keep noindex live until they drop out, then optionally 301 to bheard.in.
Self-referencing canonical on every page pointing to the https://bheard.in URL. Staging must never be the canonical.
Remove all bheard.vercel.app links from live content. The content doc's CTA and footer links (Privacy, Terms, Sitemap, #work) currently point to the Vercel domain — replace with relative or bheard.in URLs before launch.
XML sitemap at /sitemap.xml, auto-generated, listing only indexable 200-status canonical URLs. Reference it in robots.txt and submit in Google Search Console.
robots.txt: allow all on production; disallow nothing except admin/api routes. Do not block CSS/JS.
301 redirect map: map every current live bheard.in URL to its new equivalent before switchover. No 404s on previously indexed URLs.
Performance (Core Web Vitals)
Serve images in WebP/AVIF with explicit width/height attributes; lazy-load everything below the fold; the hero image/video loads eagerly with high fetch priority.
Client logo strips: single optimized sprite or individually compressed SVG/WebP files — not 25 separate PNG requests.
Self-host fonts with font-display: swap; preload the primary heading font.
No layout shift from the stats counters (10+ / 50+ / 200+) or logo carousels — reserve space in CSS.
Defer all third-party scripts (analytics, chat widget); nothing render-blocking in <head> except critical CSS.
On-page hygiene
Exactly one H1 per page; heading levels nest without skipping (H1 → H2 → H3). The content doc's visual hierarchy maps cleanly — confirm in code, not just design.
Descriptive alt text on all case-study and client imagery (e.g. “Radisson Blu Goa social media campaign creative”). Decorative flourishes get empty alt="".
Fix known live-site bugs before launch: “BEHEARD” typo in footer CTA, placeholder footer address, duplicate Tech Solutions section render.
Footer NAP (name, address, phone) must exactly match the Google Business Profile listing — character for character.
Internal linking rules
Homepage → both service pillars, /industries/hospitality-luxury, and /work. “06 / AI FOR HOSPITALITY” card links to /services/tech-solutions/ai-chatbots-agents.
Hospitality industry page ↔ AI Guest Agents page link to each other in body copy — this pair is the ranking engine; the content doc already flags the Guest AI hyperlink placement in the Radisson case study.
Every case study → its industry page + the service pages used (e.g. Radisson Blu → hospitality-luxury + brand-solutions + ai-chatbots-agents).
Every industry page → its relevant case studies under “Work.”
Anchor text: descriptive, not “click here” / “learn more” alone (e.g. “AI Guest Agents for hotels”).
Never link to innowrap.com anywhere on the site, including the guest-ai reference in the keyword notes. All tech capability is presented as in-house.
6. International Targeting (India, US, Southeast Asia)
No hreflang. One English-language site serving all markets needs no hreflang; adding it with a single language/region variant creates errors, not gains.
Signal reach through content, which the locked copy already does (“across India and international markets,” Broward College US, Caper Travel South Asia) and through Service schema areaServed values.
Google Business Profiles for the Mumbai HQ and Delhi branch, categories “Marketing agency” / “Software company,” linked to bheard.in. This is the local-pack lever for “branding agency Lower Parel”-type queries.
Keep the .in domain. It does not prevent US/SEA rankings for the service and hospitality-AI queries being targeted; a domain migration would cost more than it gains.
